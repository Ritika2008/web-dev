Classic Maggie

Ingredients

  • onions
  • corns
  • capsicum
  • tomato
  • maggie noodles
  • maggie masala
  • salt,chilli,garlic

Instructions

  1. Maggi Noodles: You will need two packets of Maggi Noodles with Maggi masala tastemaker, which are along with it.
  2. Onion and Tomato: I have used 1 each onion and tomato.
  3. Mix Vegetables: Add vegetables of your choice, like capsicum (bell peppers), sweet corn, carrot, green peas, and green beans.
  4. Ginger and garlic: I used homemade ginger garlic paste. You can also mince them and add them.
  5. Spices: I used red chili powder, garam masala powder, and chaat masala.
  6. Butter or oil: Use any vegetable oil for this Maggi. You can also use butter instead of oil.
  7. Fresh coriander: for garnishing

Additional Information

Preparation Time 10 minutes
Cooking Time 10 minutes
